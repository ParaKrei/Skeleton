--Skeleton--
--A Truly Immortal Character--

Cannot die to ANYTHING, but can still get hurt. [done]
Hates minecarts; can destroy minecart doors and get out of any minecart. [done]
Lost rings become bones. [done]
Is as fast as Sonic, but has Knuckles' jump height. [done]
Has Knuckles glide ONLY, or Metal's float. [float used]
ALWAYS attacking (hurts others on contact) [done]
Can destroy breakable walls and floors. [done]
If hit when the player has no rings, the Skeleton falls apart. [done]
The signpost image is the last frame in an animation made by jumpbreak96 (https://jumpbreak96.newgrounds.com); source: https://x.com/jumpbreak96/status/1467152269288353800 [done]
All sounds are made with a xylophone. [done]
Inhaling air bubbles will decrease gravity temporarily. [done]
Hugs from ANY character causes the skeleton to comically explode, knocking the hugger away while killing the skeleton. A unique animation for each character that can hug needs to be made for their reaction to the timer. [done]
Custom title screen like Sonic Mania Plus that puts "The Skeleton update!" in the style of Minecraft's splash text. [done]

The skeleton's visuals is a digitized 3D model of a skeleton, taken from Blender's "Human Base Meshes" pack.
The skeleton has some taunts as well:
1. The base skeleton sprite, but with a hat on, displays for only a couple of ticks; a "blink and you'll miss it" taunt.
2. The image used for the signpost poorly placed on top of the base skeleton sprite; plays the "Bad to the Bone" riff.
3. A taunt from Pizza Tower, but without any posing.
The Skeleton has ONLY ONE angle for all of these, and it's the front-facing one.
These taunts are triggered randomly upon button press.
